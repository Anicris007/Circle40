Circle40

Your friends got married. You still want to go out.

Circle40 helps single adults find people to do things with: a Saturday brunch, a movie, a weekend trek. It is for friendship, not dating. Everyone here is in the same life stage, so nobody has to explain why their weekends got quiet.

What the app does

Plans, not profiles

People post something specific they want to do, with a date and place. You join a plan instead of swiping on faces.

Small groups first

Plans cap at 2 to 6 people. Meeting a few strangers at a café feels far less awkward than a one-on-one.

Matched by interests

Suggested people share your hobbies, age range and neighbourhood, and are free on the same evenings.

Regulars

After two good meetups you can mark someone a regular, so a one-time plan can become a real friendship.

Safety built in
  • Verified profiles. Phone and photo ID check before anyone can join a plan.
  • Public places only for first meetups, with a share-my-plan option for a trusted contact.
  • Friendship mode is enforced. Romantic advances can be reported and lead to removal.
  • Women-only and men-only plans for people who feel more comfortable that way.
